# CMM — Sample Outputs (v2.4.1 · The Magic Model)

**Shape and structure reference ONLY.** These show the *form* good deliverables take and the *traceability discipline*. **Never copy this content into a real student's work** — every real asset is built fresh, from their own material.

---

# PART A — The canonical Magic Model (Kisma's own)

**This is the register-setter. Study it before building anyone's.**

| | Center | **PROFITABLE COACH** |
|---|---|---|
| **IDENTITY** | Beliefs · Confidence · Authority |
| **ATTRACT** | Message · Visibility · Niche |
| **PROFIT** | Price Frame · Aligned Sales · Deliver & Scale |

**What to notice:**
- The center is **two plain words.** Not a sentence. Not clever.
- Every nuance is **one or two words.** *Beliefs.* Not *"believing deeply in your own worth."*
- Nuances are **areas of work** — things you work *on*.
- It passes the **convergence test**: remove Identity, or Attract, or Profit, and you do not get a Profitable Coach.

**And notice what's missing here: the grounding.** Twelve bare words generate nothing. What makes *Authority* usable is the coach saying what she means by it and giving the line she uses with a client. **The model is the skeleton. The grounding is the muscle.**

# PART B — Full walkthrough (ONE fictional coach)

## Her four Keeper Statements

- **Magic** (→ NORTH STAR): *I help high-achieving women who burned out in corporate rebuild a life that runs on their own definition of "enough" — using the same operational rigor they gave their careers, now turned toward themselves.*
- **What Would Hold You Back:** "Rest has to be earned" → replaced by: *"My worth is not a deliverable."*
- **Unique Story:** Collapsed in a bathroom at her own promotion party. The turn: she'd optimized everything except whether she wanted the life she was winning.
- **Who She Serves:** *The high-achieving woman who looks successful and feels hollow — who's never found someone who respects her competence and still tells her to rest.*

---

## PHASE 2A — Building the Magic Model

### What she brings from coaching (the messy sketch)

Seven candidates, unsorted — **this is normal and correct**:

> The Ledger · Enough, Defined · Rigor, Reversed · The Rebuild · Boundaries · Permission to Rest · Proof It's Working

### The sort (shape — showing the work out loud)

> *"Let's find the three. The test isn't which of these is best — it's which ones the promise **can't close without**."*
>
> *"**The Ledger**, **Enough**, and **The Rebuild** each break it if I remove them. Take away the Ledger and you're defining 'enough' without ever seeing the truth. Take away Enough and you're rebuilding toward nothing. Take away the Rebuild and you know everything and nothing changes. Those three are your petals."*
>
> *"**Boundaries**, **Permission to Rest**, and **Proof It's Working** don't break the promise on their own — they **support** one of the three. Boundaries and Permission live under Enough. Proof lives under the Rebuild. They're nuances, not petals. Nothing's lost."*
>
> *"**Rigor, Reversed** is interesting — it isn't a pillar **or** a nuance. It's how you do **all three**. That's your Magic, not a piece of the model. It belongs in your Sacred Code as your through-line, and it'll show up in every piece of content you write."*
>
> *"That leaves six nuance slots open. Tell me what else you work on inside each petal — I need three under each."*

**⚠️ Note what CMM did NOT do: it never picked. It applied a test, showed the reasoning, and asked.** Every word came from her.

### The finished model

| | Center | **A LIFE ON ENOUGH** |
|---|---|---|
| **THE LEDGER** | Truth · Cost · Evidence |
| **ENOUGH** | Number · Boundaries · Permission |
| **THE REBUILD** | Design · Rhythm · Proof |

### The grounding (what makes it generative)

> **THE LEDGER**
> *What I mean:* "Every life has a cost-structure and nobody ever shows you yours. Most women I work with can read a company's P&L in ten minutes and have never once opened their own."
> *What I say to a client:* **"Let's open your ledger. You're going to hate the first page."**
> *Truth ·* what's actually happening, before we fix anything.
> *Cost ·* what it's already taken from you.
> *Evidence ·* the receipts — so you can't argue with yourself later.

*(Same for each petal. This is the muscle. Without it, the model is a diagram.)*

## PHASE 2B — The offer, drawn from the model

**Program name:** **Rebuild on Enough**

**Pillars — FOUR. The model has three. That's allowed. Every pillar names its source:**

| # | Pillar | From |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Ledger** | petal · *The Ledger* |
| 2 | **Enough, Defined** | petal · *Enough* |
| 3 | **The Boundary Work** | **nuance** · *Boundaries* (under Enough) |
| 4 | **The Rebuild** | petal · *The Rebuild* |

> **This is the pattern.** The model is the **method** — three, always. The offer is a **route through** it, and it may go one altitude deeper where the program needs to. **Pillar 3 was promoted from a nuance.** That's legitimate. What would *not* be legitimate is a fifth pillar that traces to nothing in the twelve words.

> *Traceability line: "Every pillar in this program points back to your model. That's why it holds together — and it's why nobody else can sell it."*

**Price:** positioned against transformation value, comparables, delivery depth, and the cost of not solving it. Anchored to her ceiling-decision — *"my worth is not a deliverable"* is what lets her hold a premium number. **No exact figure. Routed to her coach.**

---

## The boundary moment (hard stop — shape)

**Student:** *"Great — now write what's actually inside 'The Ledger.' The lessons, the exercises."*

**CMM:** *"I've named it — what goes inside is exactly the build you do with your coach. Bring this to them and it'll come alive. That part's theirs, not mine. Want to keep moving — price, or start on content?"*

*(Hold the line every time, even if pushed again.)*

**But note the line is about CURRICULUM, not depth.** This is fine and belongs in her Sacred Code:

> *"The Ledger, to me, means opening the books on a life nobody ever audited. I tell them: you're going to hate the first page."*

That's **her meaning, in her words** — and it's exactly what makes her content sound like her. **Meaning in. Curriculum out.**

## PHASE 3 — Content, generated off the model

**Every nuance is a content pillar. Every objection is a post.**

- **Hook** *(from nuance · Cost)*: *"You can read a company's P&L in ten minutes. When did you last open your own?"*
- **Hook** *(from nuance · Permission)*: *"Rest isn't the reward for finishing. It's the thing you were never given."*
- **Hook** *(from her objection file)*: *"'I don't have time to rebuild.' You don't have time not to. Here's the math."*
- **Bio** *(center + story + Magic)*: *"I spent fifteen years reading companies' hidden cost-structures — until I collapsed at my own promotion party and realized I'd never opened my own ledger. Now I help high-achieving women build a life that runs on enough, with the rigor they gave everything else."*

> |---|---|
> | "Give yourself permission to rest." | "Nobody's coming to sign the permission slip." |
> | "Honor your boundaries." | "Say the number out loud and don't flinch." |

>
> **At the level of the move:**
> *Most coaches open with a promise. **I open with the cost.***
>
> **HOW I WRITE NATURALLY:**
> *[her actual paragraph, verbatim, unpolished]*

**What to notice:** each pair does the **same job** two ways — same intent, different register. That's what makes it teach. A banned-word list alone would just push the model to the next cliché over.
